Graduation Project Shipped AI-Integrated

AINAI — AI Fashion E-Commerce Platform

AinaiGP/Ainai-Backend · Backend Architecture Lead

A full-scale, AI-integrated fashion e-commerce platform built for the MENA region, where online shoppers can virtually try on clothing before they buy — uploading a photo and seeing how a garment looks on their own body in seconds. The platform solves one of the biggest pain points in online fashion: buying clothes without knowing how they'll actually look on you.

Under the hood it's a modular monolithic NestJS backend designed for production — event-driven via AWS SQS, Redis-backed for all ephemeral state, and wired to two live AI feature integrations: a Gemini-powered shopping assistant that understands your catalog and a virtual try-on pipeline proxied from a FastAPI AI model. Built, tested, and shipped as a graduation project at El Shorouk Academy.

Virtual try-on — multi-phase upload state machine, NestJS proxy to FastAPI AI model, Cloudinary URL matching
Gemini chatbot — product-aware context injection, floating UI, real catalog knowledge
Redis cart — anonymous→auth merge on login, atomic inventory reservation with race condition protection
Fuzzy search — PostgreSQL pg_trgm with GIN indexes, multi-filter, cursor-based pagination
JWT + Google OAuth, RBAC, refresh token rotation in Redis with TTL
AWS SQS event queue — decoupled order processing, inventory updates, notification dispatch
